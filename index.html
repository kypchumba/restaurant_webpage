About the dish

It is a popular coastal dish in Kenya that is made from fish that is cooked in a spicy sauce.
The dish typically uses tilapia or red snapper, which is first marinated in ablend of spices such as turmeric, coriander and cumin then cooked in a mixture of coconut milk, tomatoes, onions, garlic and chilli peppers.
This dish goes at a price of Ksh.1700

About the dish

This dish is popular in Chinese cuisine and it involves a roasted duck dish that is known for its crispy skin and tender meat. Pecking duck is often served with thin pancakes, scallions, cucumber and hoisin sauce.
This dish is priced at only Ksh.2000

About this dish

Nyama choma is a popular dish in East Africa, particularly in Kenya. It consists of grilled/roasted meat(mostly goat or beef) Nyama choma is a swahili word that translates to roasted meat.
The dish is typically marinated with a mixture of spices, including salt, peeper and some cumin or coriander. It is often searved with ugali, a cornmeal-based dish and some veges or salad.
This special dish is priced at Ksh.1000 per kilogram of meat.
