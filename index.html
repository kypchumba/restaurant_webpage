About the dish

About the dish

About this dish

Nyama choma is a popular dish in East Africa, particularly in Kenya. It consists of grilled/roasted meat(mostly goat or beef) Nyama choma is a swahili word that translates to roasted meat.
The dish is typically marinated with a mixture of spices, including salt, peeper and some cumin or coriander. It is often searved with ugali, a cornmeal-based dish and some veges or salad.
This special dish is priced at Ksh.1000 per kilogram of meat.
